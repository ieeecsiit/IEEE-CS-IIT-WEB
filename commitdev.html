About commit.dev

Commit.dev is an upcoming year-long initiative tailored exclusively for IEEE Computer Society global members at IIT, focused on driving engagement with our members. Set to launch soon, the program will feature bi-monthly meetups, each comprising two technical sessions, one networking session, exciting swag giveaways, and a variety of engaging activities. Designed to foster continuous learning, professional development, and community-building, Commit.dev aims to create a vibrant space where members can collaborate, exchange knowledge, and expand their network.
