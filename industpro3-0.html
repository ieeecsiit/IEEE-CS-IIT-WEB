IndustPro 3.0

About IndustPro

IndustPro 3.0 was designed to boost professional development and industry readiness, especially targeting students preparing for their placement year. The program featured four workshops, including CV building, LinkedIn profile optimization, effective career fair preparation, open-source contributions, and securing summer internships. Enriched by tech talks from six seasoned industry professionals, each session drew over 300 participants. IndustPro 3.0 offered a valuable platform for students to gain practical knowledge, strengthen their professional profiles, and confidently navigate the path toward successful careers and internships.
