About StackMedium

StackMedium is a year-long monthly blog initiative aimed at helping undergraduates sharpen their technical writing skills while sharing their knowledge with a wider audience. Each month, participants can submit articles on a range of tech-related topics, which will be published on the official Medium platform. To encourage quality and creativity, one standout article is selected monthly based on set criteria and recognized as the winner. This creates a collaborative space for students to grow as writers, gain exposure, and actively contribute to the tech community.


StackMedium has honoured these inspiring writers so far as the monthly winners:


  • December 2025 Winner: Lidiya Rajapakse for his insightful article “Did You Know? Multimodal AI Is Teaching Machines to See, Hear, and Understand Together,” which explores how multimodal AI enables machines to process and understand multiple forms of data simultaneously.

  • November 2025 Winner: Hirushi Jayasekara for her article “Who Is Driving Your Home Robot,” which offers a sharp, forward-looking perspective on how friendly home robots quietly reshape trust and privacy, and why clear governance is essential.

  • October 2025 Winner: Biyoni Pietersz for her article “AI in 2025: Rethinking How We Innovate and Create,” which presents a sharp, forward-looking view on how artificial intelligence is transforming creativity and innovation.
