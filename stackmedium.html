About StackMedium

StackMedium is a year-long monthly blog initiative aimed at helping undergraduates sharpen their technical writing skills while sharing their knowledge with a wider audience. Each month, participants can submit articles on a range of tech-related topics, which will be published on the official Medium platform. To encourage quality and creativity, one standout article is selected monthly based on set criteria and recognized as the winner. This ongoing project creates a collaborative space for students to grow as writers, gain exposure, and actively contribute to the tech community.


Recognition

Recognition is given to the best article each month, which is published on the official Medium platform.
