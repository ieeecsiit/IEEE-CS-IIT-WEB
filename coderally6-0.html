About CodeRally 6.0


Competitive Programming Workshop

An introductory workshop designed to introduce participants to the fundamentals of competitive programming, effective problem-solving approaches, and commonly used contest structures.


Algorithm Fundamentals Workshop

This session focused on essential algorithmic topics such as sorting techniques, searching methods, and recursion, helping participants build a strong conceptual base.


Advanced Algorithm Techniques Workshop

Participants explored advanced problem-solving approaches including dynamic programming and greedy algorithms, enabling them to handle more challenging computational problems.


Advanced Programming Workshop

A deep dive into advanced coding practices, performance optimization, and language-specific strategies aimed at improving efficiency in competitive programming scenarios.


Competitive Programming Guide Workshop

This workshop offered hands-on guidance and contest-oriented strategies such as effective time management, debugging techniques, and analyzing edge cases.


24-hour Hackathon Awareness Session

An orientation session that prepared participants for the hackathon by covering rules, expectations, team coordination, and effective project planning methods.


24-hour Hackathon

The flagship event of CodeRally 6.0, this 24-hour hackathon pushed participants to transform ideas into innovative technical solutions within a limited timeframe. The event emphasized collaboration, creativity, and problem-solving under pressure, giving teams a platform to showcase both technical skills and teamwork.
