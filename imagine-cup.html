Microsoft Imagine Cup Awareness Session

About the event

We hosted a highly engaging Microsoft Imagine Cup Awareness session that attracted over 300 enthusiastic attendees. The session featured an insightful keynote by Mr. Prabhath Mannapperuma, who provided an in-depth overview of the Imagine Cup competition, its global significance, and how students can leverage it to showcase their innovative ideas on an international stage.


Team Nana Shilpa took the stage to share their championship-winning strategies and experiences offering attendees firsthand insights into building impactful tech solutions.


In addition, Mr. Nandun Diaz introduced the audience to the Microsoft Learn platform, outlining how students can access structured learning paths, certifications, and resources to upskill and stay industry-ready continuously.


This session not only raised awareness about one of the world's most prestigious student tech competitions but also empowered participants with practical knowledge and tools to pursue innovation and personal growth within the tech industry.
