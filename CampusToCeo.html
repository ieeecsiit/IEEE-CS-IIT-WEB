About Campus To CEO

Kithsiri Gunasekara, Director of VSIS, delivered the Campus to CEO session organized by the IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter of IIT. The event provided a valuable opportunity for students from IIT as well as participants from several other universities. Through initiatives such as this, VSIS and the broader group remain committed to knowledge sharing and empowering the next generation to contribute to a brighter future for the nation.
